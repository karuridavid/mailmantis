HOW IT WORKS

From cold domain to trusted sender.

Inbox providers trust domains that send ordinary mail people open and reply to. Mail Mantis creates exactly that, in four steps, and shows you the result for every email.

Connect

Add your domain’s SMTP sender and test it. Connect Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud or IMAP inboxes you own, or invite friends to lend theirs.

Brief

AI reads your public website and drafts a business brief. You correct it, or write it yourself.

Draft & send

Get a different, ordinary email for each inbox. Edit, mark ready and send, or let autopilot send a few each day.

Check & converse

See Primary, Promotions or Spam for every send. Reply from the seed inbox, then answer back from your domain in the same thread.

Bring your own AI

Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq or Cloudflare Workers AI with your own key, or paste the prompt into any chat app. Emails come from a brief you approved, with no invented offers.

Two-way conversations

Reply from the seed inbox, then answer back from your domain, all in the same thread. AI drafts each reply from the conversation so far.

Not spam, important, filters

Move an email out of spam or mark it important when you choose. One click adds a Gmail filter that skips Spam and marks your mail important.

Autopilot

A daily run that sends a few new emails and moves each conversation on by one reply. Choose 1 to 10 emails a day, run it now, or pause it any time.

THE EMAILS VIEW

Every email, from draft to reply.

A mail-client layout with colour-coded status. Open any email to edit it, send it, see its placement and timeline, or continue the conversation.

  • Draft → Ready → Sent, with a confirmation before anything leaves
  • Placement checked automatically while the dashboard is open, with a clear warning when an email never arrived
  • Replies from the seed and from your domain, threaded together
  • Keyboard friendly: J/K to move, N for new emails

SELF-HOST

Free to run. Up in three commands.

Docker Compose starts Postgres and the app, generates the encryption key on first boot, and runs autopilot’s daily schedule itself. Add HTTPS for a public domain with one more flag, or deploy to Vercel with Neon.

For your own domains and inboxes

Mail Mantis sends a handful of emails a day, only to inboxes you own or that friends lend you. It is not a bulk sender or an outreach tool, and it can’t guarantee inbox placement. Good authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and real engagement matter far more than any tool.
